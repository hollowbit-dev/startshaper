Startshaper finds out why your computer is slow. It lists every app that starts by itself, explains in plain words what each one is, and lets you switch off the ones you don't need. Every change can be undone with one click.

**What's in 1.0.0**
- **Startup:** every app and background helper that starts by itself, sorted into *Safe to turn off*, *Your call* and *Leave these on*.
- **On Windows:** your startup time in seconds, read from Windows' own startup log (run as administrator to see it), and how much each app adds to it.
- **Right now:** which apps are using the most memory and processor, grouped by app.
- **Changes:** everything you switched off, with *Turn everything back on*.
- Nothing is ever deleted. Switching off uses the same setting as Task Manager's Startup tab (Windows) or Login Items (Mac). No registry tweaks, no services stopped.
- Free, no account, no ads. The only network request is a daily update check you can turn off.

**Downloads**
- Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer): `Startshaper-1.0.0-mac-arm64.dmg`
- Mac with Intel: `Startshaper-1.0.0-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10/11: `Startshaper-Setup-1.0.0-win-x64.exe`

The apps aren't signed yet. On a Mac, right-click Startshaper and choose Open the first time. On Windows, click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
