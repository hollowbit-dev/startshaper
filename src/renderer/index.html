Why is my PC slow?

Startshaper finds the apps that start by themselves and slow your computer down, tells you in plain words what each one is, and lets you switch them off. Every change can be undone with one click.

Takes a few seconds. Changes nothing until you say so.
  • Reading what starts by itself
  • Measuring memory and processor use
  • Checking how long startup takes
